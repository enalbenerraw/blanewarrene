# AI SMB Toolkit

Four Word documents that turn AI curiosity into a working plan for executives and operators at companies with 25 to 150 employees. Covers platform selection, employee discovery, vertical-specific workflows, and self-serve course building.

Created by Blane Warrene.

## What's inside

| Document | What it does |
|---|---|
| [Tech Stack Inventory](deliverable-1-tech-stack-inventory.docx) | Structured inventory to map your current tools and where AI platforms fit |
| [HR AI Survey](deliverable-2-hr-ai-survey.docx) | Employee discovery survey to find where AI is already being used (and where it should be) |
| [Prompt Cookbook](deliverable-3-prompt-cookbook.docx) | Vertical-specific prompt workflows ready to customize for your company |
| [Course Builder](deliverable-4-course-builder.docx) | Self-serve framework for building an internal AI enablement course |

Each is a standalone Word document, ready to customize: add your company name, fill in your tools, use immediately. No technical setup, no subscriptions, no consultants required.

## Quick start

1. Download the four `.docx` files above.
2. Start with the Tech Stack Inventory to baseline your current tools.
3. Run the HR AI Survey to find where AI adoption already exists inside your team.
4. Use the Prompt Cookbook to build workflows for your specific vertical.
5. Use the Course Builder to turn the above into an internal enablement course.
