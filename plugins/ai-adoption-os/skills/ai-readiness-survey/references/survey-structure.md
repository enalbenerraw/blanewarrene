# Survey structure

Under ten questions. Behavior over attitude. One free-text.

```
# AI Readiness Survey: [Company Name]

**Prepared:** [date]
**Audience:** [headcount] people across [functions]
**Sent by:** [name or role]
**Open until:** [date]

---

## Preamble to send with the survey

[Plain words, addressed to employees. States: what this is for, what will be done
with the answers, whether it is anonymous, and that admitting to unsanctioned tool
use will not get anyone in trouble. If anonymity cannot be guaranteed by the
tooling, say so here instead of promising it.]

---

## The questions

1. **[Behavior question.]**
   - [option] / [option] / [option]
   - *Reads as:* [what each answer tells you]

[Repeat. Keep under ten. Tune options by function where it matters.]

**Free text:** What task takes you the longest that you suspect a machine could
help with?

---

## Distribution plan

- **Send in:** [tool]
- **Intro message:** [two sentences, from the named sender]
- **Open for:** [duration]
- **Reminder:** [when, and what it says]
- **What people are told happens next:** [specific]

---

## Reading the results

| If you see | It means | Do this |
|------------|----------|---------|

Include at least one row whose action is **do nothing**. A rubric that always
recommends action is not a rubric.

---

*Created by Blane Warrene, blanewarrene.com*
```
